## Sent (pedal → controller)

> No published documentation exists for the status SysEx emitted by the pedal; the decoding below is the result of my own capture and analysis.

A 27-byte SysEx message is emitted automatically every 3.5 seconds.

Typical captured message:

```
F0 00 20 1F 00 55 56 01 01 06 00 00 01 4C 4B 71 46 50 50 7F 4B 00 33 36 32 00 F7
```

| Byte #   | Raw value(s)     | Role | Comments |
|----------|------------------|------|------------------|
| `0`      | `0xF0`           | Start of SysEx | *Standard* |
| `1`      | `0x00`           | Manufacturer ID | *Standard*<br>3-byte Manufacturer ID<br/>(first byte) |
| `2`      | `0x20`           | Manufacturer ID<br/>(second byte) | *Standard*<br>`European Manufacturer` |
| `3`      | `0x1F`           | Manufacturer ID<br/>(third byte)  | *Standard*<br>`TC Electronic` |
| `4`      | `0x00`           | Device ID | *Standard* |
| `5`      | `0x55`           | Model ID? (Constant) | *Start of proprietary payload*<br/>*Role inferred from the TC Electronic M-ONE, D-TWO & C-THREE MIDI specification* |
| `6`      | `0x56`           | Message type? (Constant) | *Role inferred from the TC Electronic M-ONE, D-TWO & C-THREE MIDI specification* |
| `7`–`8`  | `0x01 0x01`      | ? (Constant) | — |
| `9`      | `0x06`           | Preset | Displays:<br/>`0x00`: `--`<br/>`0x01`–`0x12` (1-18):<br/>`1`–`18` in normal and toggle mode<br/>*or*<br/>`A`-`I` `1`-`2` in bank mode |
| `10`      | `0x00`          | Overdrive (on/off) | `0x00` (0): off<br/>`0x01` (1): on |
| `11`      | `0x00`          | Distortion (on/off) | `0x00` (0): off<br/>`0x01` (1): on |
| `12`      | `0x01`          | Routing | `0x00` (0): OD→DS (serial)<br/>`0x01` (1): DS→OD (serial)<br/>`0x02` (2): OD∥DS (parallel) |
| `13`      | `0x4C`          | DS gain| `0x00`–`0x7F` (0-127) |
| `14`      | `0x4B`          | Bass | `0x00`–`0x7F` (0-127) |
| `15`      | `0x71`          | Treble | `0x00`–`0x7F` (0-127) |
| `16`      | `0x46`          | Mix | `0x00`–`0x7F` (0-127) |
| `17`      | `0x50`          | DS level | `0x00`–`0x7F` (0-127) |
| `18`      | `0x50`          | OD level | `0x00`–`0x7F` (0-127) |
| `19`      | `0x7F`          | Tone | `0x00`–`0x7F` (0-127) |
| `20`      | `0x4B`          | OD gain | `0x00`–`0x7F` (0-127) |
| `21`      | `0x00`          | Switch mode | `0x00` (0): normal<br/>`0x01` (1): toggle<br/>`0x02` (2): bank |
| `22`–`25` | `0x33 0x36 0x32 0x00` | ? (Constant) | *End of proprietary payload* |
| `26`      | `0xF7`          | End of SysEx | *Standard* |

## Received (controller → pedal)

**All messages on Channel 2.**

| Preset    | PC    (`0xC2`)      | `0x00`–`0x11` (1–18) |

| OD gain   | CC 23 (`0xB2 0x17`) | `0x00`–`0x7F` |
| OD level  | CC 24 (`0xB2 0x18`) | `0x00`–`0x7F` |
| OD tone   | CC 25 (`0xB2 0x19`) | `0x00`–`0x7F` |
